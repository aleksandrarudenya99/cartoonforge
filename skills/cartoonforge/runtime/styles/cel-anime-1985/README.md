# Cel Anime · 1980s

Hand-painted cels, airbrushed sunset, striped retro sun, glowing city lights.

**Lineage:** 1980s Japanese TV and film anime on painted cels.

## What defines the look
- Flat colours + ONE hard cel-shadow
- Airbrushed sunset gradient sky
- Bloom on lights (`bloom`), film grain
- Power lines and skyline silhouettes

## Sound
- Music: generated `citypop` at 104 bpm, treated with `vhs` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `vhs` treatment, speed 1.0

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No soft 3D shading
- Don't: No modern digital glow overload

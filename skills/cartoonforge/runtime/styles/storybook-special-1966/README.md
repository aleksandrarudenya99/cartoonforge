# Storybook TV Special · 1960s

Impossibly curvy leaning houses, scratchy pen line, spiral snow, rhymes.

**Lineage:** Genre look of 1960s holiday TV specials adapted from rhyming picture books (not any specific special).

## What defines the look
- Curvy leaning bulb-shaped houses and a spiral mountain
- Scratchy boiling pen line, limited flat palette
- 8 drawings/s, held comic poses
- Spiral snowflakes

## Sound
- Music: generated `whimsy` at 108 bpm, treated with `tape` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `storyteller` treatment, speed 1.0

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No green grumps, Who-towns, reindeer-dogs or text from existing books

## Legal
Styles, eras and techniques are free to use. Never reproduce characters, logos, titles, signature props or settings of an existing work, even if the user asks for "100% like X" — offer this genre look instead.

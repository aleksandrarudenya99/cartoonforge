# Primetime TV Cartoon · 1990s

Bright flat colours, even thick outlines, suburban street, a broadcast feel.

**Lineage:** Genre look of 90s prime-time TV animation (not any specific series).

## What defines the look
- Flat bright fills, even thick dark-brown outlines
- Suburban houses, lawn, sidewalk, fence
- Slight chroma bleed + scanlines
- Character colours come from the user

## Sound
- Music: generated `sitcom` at 128 bpm, treated with `broadcast` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `tv` treatment, speed 1.02

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No 'house' yellow skin tone, no famous families, couches or towns
- Don't: No show logos or fonts

## Legal
Styles, eras and techniques are free to use. Never reproduce characters, logos, titles, signature props or settings of an existing work, even if the user asks for "100% like X" — offer this genre look instead.

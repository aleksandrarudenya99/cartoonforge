# Zany TV Cartoon · late 1990s

Wobbly lively outlines, odd proportions, acid colours, everything breathes.

**Lineage:** Genre look of late-90s cable TV cartoons (not any specific series).

## What defines the look
- Thick wobbling outlines (`boil` with big scale)
- Saturated, slightly-off colours, painted texture
- Tilted wonky buildings that squash on the beat
- Extreme squash & stretch

## Sound
- Music: generated `zany` at 138 bpm, treated with `broadcast` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `cartoon` treatment, speed 1.06

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No underwater towns, sponge/starfish characters, football-head kids or city blocks from existing series

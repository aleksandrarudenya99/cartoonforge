# Saturday Morning Cartoon · 1980s

Clean TV cels, bold primaries, airbrushed skies, harbour adventures.

**Lineage:** Genre look of 1980s TV adventure cartoons (not any specific series).

## What defines the look
- Clean black line, bold primary fills, simple cel-shadow
- Airbrushed sunset sky
- Adventure settings: harbour, docks, city
- Soft broadcast picture

## Sound
- Music: generated `saturday` at 126 bpm, treated with `vhs` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `tv` treatment, speed 1.02

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No turtles with masks, sewer lairs, duck families or mansions from existing series

## Legal
Styles, eras and techniques are free to use. Never reproduce characters, logos, titles, signature props or settings of an existing work, even if the user asks for "100% like X" — offer this genre look instead.

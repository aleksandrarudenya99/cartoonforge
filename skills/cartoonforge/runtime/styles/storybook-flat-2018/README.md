# Storybook Flat Adventure · 2010s

Flat picture-book shapes, limited palette, Nordic mountains and a gentle giant.

**Lineage:** Genre look of flat illustrative adventure series inspired by picture books (not any specific series).

## What defines the look
- Flat geometric mountains and pines, no outline on scenery
- Limited palette, paper-print texture
- A giant soft creature silhouette on the horizon
- Thin-line characters

## Sound
- Music: generated `nordic` at 100 bpm, treated with `tape` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `storyteller` treatment, speed 1.0

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No blue-haired kids, deer-foxes, elves or trolls from existing books/series

## Legal
Styles, eras and techniques are free to use. Never reproduce characters, logos, titles, signature props or settings of an existing work, even if the user asks for "100% like X" — offer this genre look instead.

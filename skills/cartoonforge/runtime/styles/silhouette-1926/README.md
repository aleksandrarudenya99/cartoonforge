# Silhouette Theatre · 1920s

Black cut-paper puppets with lace edges against a glowing backdrop. A fairy-tale waltz.

**Lineage:** Shadow-puppet theatre and 1920s silhouette film (Germany).

## What defines the look
- Characters and scenery are pure black cut-outs; eyes and mouth are holes the light shows through
- Glowing radial backdrop: gold → amber → rose → indigo
- Ornament: curls, vines, lace edges (`cutEdge`)
- Depth from lighter far layers, not from shading

## Sound
- Music: generated `waltz` at 96 bpm, treated with `hall` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `storyteller` treatment, speed 0.98

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No colour inside the silhouettes
- Don't: No outlines — the edge IS the drawing

# Ink Wash · 1960s

Brush and ink on rice paper: soft bleeding wash, mountains in mist, a red seal.

**Lineage:** Chinese ink-wash animation, Shanghai, from 1960.

## What defines the look
- Grey ink gradations on rice paper, no outlines
- Ink bleeds into the paper (`inkBleed`)
- Mountains in mist, lots of empty space
- A red seal stamp as the only colour

## Sound
- Music: generated `pentatonic` at 72 bpm, treated with `mist` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `airy` treatment, speed 0.95

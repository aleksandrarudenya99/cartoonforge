# Mid-Century Modern · 1950s

Flat colour shapes, line drawn off the colour, atomic starbursts, cool jazz.

**Lineage:** 1950s American modernist studios that rebelled against realism.

## What defines the look
- Flat colour fields, no gradients
- Thin line drawn slightly OFF the colour (misregistered layers)
- Atomic-age motifs: starbursts, boomerangs
- Limited animation: 8 drawings/s, poses snap on the beat

## Sound
- Music: generated `cooljazz` at 132 bpm, treated with `tape` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `radio` treatment, speed 1.04

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No soft shading or 3D volume
- Don't: No busy backgrounds — big shapes, lots of air

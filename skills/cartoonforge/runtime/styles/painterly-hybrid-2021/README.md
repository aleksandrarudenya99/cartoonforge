# Painterly Hybrid · 2020s

Brush strokes painted over volume, graphic shadows, neon in smoky industrial dusk.

**Lineage:** Genre look of 2020s painterly 2D/3D hybrid series (not any specific series).

## What defines the look
- Visible brush texture over everything
- Hard graphic shadow sides on forms
- Neon signs vs smoky dusk gradient
- Hand-drawn sparks and smoke over volume

## Sound
- Music: generated `hybrid` at 96 bpm, treated with `cinema` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `hero` treatment, speed 0.98

# Comic Strip TV Special · 1960s–80s

Thin pen line, flat muted colours, one horizon line, big sky, gentle snow.

**Lineage:** Genre look of TV specials adapted from newspaper comic strips (not any specific strip).

## What defines the look
- Thin uneven pen line, simple dot eyes
- Flat muted colours, single horizon line, big empty sky
- Frozen pond, tiny tree, brick wall
- Limited animation, deadpan holds, jazz trio

## Sound
- Music: generated `cooljazz` at 120 bpm, treated with `tape` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `tv` treatment, speed 0.98

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No round-headed kids, beagles, lasagna cats or strip characters from existing comics

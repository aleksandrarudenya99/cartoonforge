# Paper Cut-out · 1950s–60s

Torn construction paper, brass fastener joints, layers casting little shadows.

**Lineage:** Czech, Soviet and Chinese (jianzhi) cut-out films; later TV cut-out comedy.

## What defines the look
- Flat paper colours with torn edges (`ridge` with jag)
- Brass paper-fastener dots at joints
- Every layer casts a small soft shadow (`paperShadow`)
- Jerky pinned motion, ~10 drawings/s

## Sound
- Music: generated `polka` at 116 bpm, treated with `none` (see `scripts/lib/audio/`)
- Voice: `storyteller` treatment, speed 1.0

## Do / don't
- Do draw the user's own characters with this style's `skin` (outline, fills, shading mode).
- Don't: No outlines and no gradients
- Don't: Parts rotate around pins; they don't bend
